BruinRep Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026 · App ID com.skratchkrafts.bruinrep

In short
  • BruinRep works without an account. Your training stays on your phone.
  • An account is optional. With one, you can keep cloud backups and sync between your devices.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can delete your account and its cloud data from inside the app at any time.

What BruinRep stores on your phone

Workouts and sets, routines and folders, programmes, exercise notes, custom exercises, body measurements, progress photos, your food log, the details behind your calorie targets (year of birth, height, weight, activity and goal), and, if you set them in You → Training, the figure the recovery map draws (broader, neutral or curvier) and the version of the calorie formula to use (male, female or in between). BruinRep never asks for your gender. These choices, and your other settings, are kept on the phone. They are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Android's own device backup (to your Google account) may include this data, so it can be restored on a new phone. Progress photos are excluded from it. Uninstalling BruinRep deletes everything stored on the phone.

If you create an account

You can sign up with an email address and password, or continue with Google.

  • Email and password: we store your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our hosting provider's sign-in service and are never visible to us.
  • Continue with Google: Google shares your email address, name, Google account ID and profile picture link with our sign-in service, which stores them with your account. BruinRep uses only your email address, to show which account you are signed in to. We never see your Google password.

Your sign-in is kept on your phone in encrypted storage.

With an account you can choose to:

  • Back up to the cloud. Each backup is a private copy of your training data (not your progress photos), made only when you ask.
  • Sync across devices. When you turn sync on, finished workouts, routines and folders, exercise notes, custom exercises, body measurements, programmes and settings (including the figure and calorie formula you chose in You → Training) are kept in your account so your other devices can receive them. Progress photos and a workout in progress are not synced.

Only you can read your cloud data. It is protected by access rules that limit each account to its own records, and it travels encrypted (HTTPS).

Health Connect (Android, optional)

If you allow it, BruinRep uses Health Connect to:

WritesFinished workouts (type, time and distance) and bodyweight you log in BruinRep
ReadsBodyweight recorded by other apps, such as Samsung Health, and your daily step counts (from your phone, watch and fitness apps)

Bodyweight read from Health Connect is stored in BruinRep like any weight you log. If you use sync or cloud backups, it is included in them. Step counts are read when you look at them and shown on your phone; BruinRep doesn't store them and they are not part of sync or backups. They leave your phone only if you switch on sharing steps with a training group (below). If you also allow "Update group steps while closed", BruinRep reads your step count for today and the two days before about once an hour in the background and sends it to those groups; you can turn this off in Health Connect. Your daily step goal is a setting, synced and backed up like your other settings. Health Connect data is used only to show your progress in BruinRep and, if you choose, your step count to your training groups. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone else.

BruinRep's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can withdraw access at any time in Health Connect's settings or by turning it off in BruinRep.

Training groups (optional, needs an account)

You can start a training group or join one with an invite code from a friend. For each group you choose a name to show and whether it sees:

StepsToday's step count and your daily goal (from Health Connect). Off until you switch it on.
WorkoutsWorkouts you finish that day (name, length, number of sets, and each exercise with the weights, reps, times or distances of its completed sets, so group members can view and copy it), new personal records and your training streak.

You can also share a routine as a template: with one group, or on BruinRep's public template feed, where anyone signed in can see, search, upvote and save it. A shared template carries the routine's exercises and targets, the name and description you give it, and a display name you choose — never your logged workouts. You can remove a template you shared at any time; copies people saved stay theirs. Anyone can report a template, and reported templates are hidden.

To show these to your group, BruinRep sends that day's figures to its servers, and only while you are in a group that sees them. Group members can also send you cheers. Only members of the group see what you share with it; nobody else can see it, and nothing else from BruinRep is shared. Change what a group sees or leave it at any time: when you leave your last group, your shared stats are deleted. Deleting your account deletes all of it.

Wear OS watch (optional)

If you install BruinRep on a Wear OS watch, the watch shows the workout in progress on your phone and sends the changes you make on the watch back to it. This goes directly between your own phone and watch over Wear OS's connection, not through BruinRep's servers.

With the Physical activity permission, the watch also counts today's steps using the watch's own step counting (Health Services). The count stays on the watch; only your step goal comes from the phone.

Camera, photos and notifications

The camera and photo picker are used when you add a progress photo, scan a food barcode or log a meal from a photo. Progress photos stay on your phone. Barcode scanning happens on the phone; only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product. Notifications are used for the rest timer and are created on the phone.

Logging a meal from a photo (Pro, optional, needs an account)

When you choose Take a photo in the food log, a smaller copy of that one photo is sent through BruinRep's server to Anthropic, whose AI model (Claude) estimates the foods and portions. BruinRep does not store the photo, and the estimate is shown to you to check and change before anything is logged. Anthropic processes the photo as our service provider, under its commercial terms, which don't allow it to train its models on what we send. To keep this fair, our server counts how many photos each account sends per day (the count only, never the photo), and deletes the counts after a week. You're told this before your first photo.

Ads (free version only)

The free version shows a few ads, as cards in lists such as your workout history, the exercise library and the template feed — never during a workout. They are provided by Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads, Google's ads software on your phone collects your device's advertising ID, a rough location from your IP address, and how you interact with the ads, under Google's own policy. Where the law requires it, you're asked first whether you want personalised ads, and you can change this in You → Account. BruinRep never gives the ads software your workouts, steps, body measurements or anything from Health Connect, and never uses that data for advertising. BruinRep Pro has no ads, and the ads software doesn't start for Pro users.

BruinRep Pro (optional purchase)

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns from Google Play whether your account has Pro. If you're signed in to a BruinRep account, the app also sends the purchase's identifier (a token from Google Play) to our server, which asks Google whether it's valid and keeps it with your account, along with which plan it is and when it ends, so features that run on our server (meal photos) know you have Pro. It is deleted with your account. Cancel a subscription any time in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Where your data is kept

Account and cloud data are hosted by Supabase, our service provider, in the European Union (Ireland). Supabase processes it only to provide BruinRep's sign-in, backups and sync. If you choose Continue with Google, Google handles that sign-in under its own privacy policy. Purchases go through Google Play, and ads in the free version come from Google AdMob (above). Meal photos are analysed by Anthropic (above), and barcode lookups use the open Open Food Facts database, which receives only the barcode number. We use no other third-party services.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Cloud data is kept until you delete it. Deleting your account in BruinRep (You → Account → Delete account) immediately deletes your cloud backups, your synced data and your sign-in. Data on your devices is not touched. See Delete your BruinRep account, which also explains how to ask us to delete it if you can no longer use the app.

Your choices and rights

You can export your workouts as a CSV file and save a full backup file at any time from the app. You can correct your data in the app, turn sync and Health Connect off, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and to complain to a data-protection authority. To make a request, contact us below.

Children

BruinRep is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
