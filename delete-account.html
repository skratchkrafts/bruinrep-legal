Delete your BruinRep account

BruinRep · App ID com.skratchkrafts.bruinrep

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open BruinRep and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to You → Account → Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, tap Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

Deletion happens straight away.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud backups
  • Your synced data in the cloud: workouts, routines and folders, exercise notes, custom exercises, body measurements, programmes and settings
  • Your sign-in: your email address and password, or the link to your Google account and the details Google shared when you signed in

What is kept

Nothing is kept in the cloud. Data on your phones and tablets stays on them, because it never needed an account. To remove it too, uninstall BruinRep from each device. If you want a copy first, save a backup file or export a CSV from You → Data and devices before deleting.

If you can't use the app

Email skratch56@skratchkrafts.com from the address on your account, with the subject “Delete my BruinRep account”. We will delete the same data within 30 days and confirm by email. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address so no one else can delete your account.

See also the BruinRep Privacy Policy.
